Features

Everything you need on-site

From the camera shutter to the final report — FieldSnap handles it all so you can focus on the job.

📷

Smart Photo Scanning

Point your camera at any equipment label. FieldSnap captures the image and sends it to AI for instant, accurate analysis.

🤖

AI Data Extraction

Powered by Google Gemini. Automatically reads model numbers, serial numbers, voltage, power ratings and more — in seconds.

📁

Organised Projects

Group scans into folders by site, client, or date. Find any piece of equipment in seconds, even months later.

☁️

Cloud Sync

All your data syncs securely to the cloud automatically. Access your scans from any device, any time.

📋

25+ Equipment Templates

Pre-built templates for electrical panels, HVAC, motors, solar inverters, power systems, telecom equipment and more.

📄

PDF Report Export

Generate clean, professional PDF reports from any folder. Share directly with clients or attach to job tickets.

🔗

Shareable Links

Share individual scans or entire project folders via a link — no app needed for the recipient to view.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Instant alerts when AI extraction completes, syncs finish, or scans are saved — so you always know where things stand.

How it works

Three steps to a perfect record

No manual data entry. No spreadsheets. No rekeying data at the office. Just point, scan, and save.

1

Choose a template

Select the equipment type — electrical panel, HVAC unit, motor, solar inverter, or one of 20+ more templates.

2

Photograph the label

Take a photo of the equipment nameplate. AI reads all the key data fields instantly — no manual typing needed.

3

Review & save

Confirm the extracted values, add notes if needed, and save to your project folder. Done in under 30 seconds.

4

Export & share

Generate a PDF report or share a link to any folder. Your data, your format, ready when the job is done.
